Bringing Quality Goods From Our Home To Yours

Welcome to Luca's Seacoast Market, your go-to place for handcrafted candles, pure Maple Syrup, farm-fresh Vegetables, and vibrant Plants. We only sell our maple syrup in the Spring due to that being the only maple syrup season. We hope that you support local artisans and producers, ensuring that you get the finest products with a touch of community love.

Some of our products include Maple Syrup (only in the spring), Coffee, Candles, and Apple Cider. To purchase products from Luca's Seacoast Market you will need to email or text us with your order.
